Watercolor Mixing Guide

Learn how to mix clean, vibrant watercolor shades without creating muddy colors.

🎨 Basic Color Mixing

Watercolor uses transparent layers. Start with primary colors: Red, Blue, and Yellow. Mix them gently on a palette before applying to paper.

✅ Correct Technique

  • Use clean water
  • Mix on palette first
  • Layer gradually
  • Let paint dry between layers

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Too much water (washed out colors)
  • Overmixing (muddy brown result)
  • Using dirty brush water
